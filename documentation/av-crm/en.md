## [id: avcrm-overview, type: reference] What is AV-CRM?
AV-CRM is a CRM software primarily used to record support issues/tickets, manage them, and track them until they are resolved.

## [id: avcrm-eligibility, type: reference] Who is allowed to create an issue/ticket in AV-CRM?
Public users cannot create a new issue/ticket in AV-CRM themselves. Only users who are valid, registered users in AVSSO are able to create issues/tickets on their own (see "avcrm-non-avsso-support" for what public/non-registered users can do instead).

## [id: avcrm-create-issue-steps, type: reference] How do I create a new issue/ticket in AV-CRM?
1. Visit https://crm.ansarvdp.gov.bd/.
2. Click the **Get Support** button in the top navigation menu bar.
3. Enter your mobile number, then enter the OTP you receive.
4. What happens next depends on your registration status:
   - **If you are not already registered in AV-CRM:** a page appears with your NID Number, Name, and MSISDN already filled in. You need to enter your email address and a password (minimum 8 characters) to create your AV-CRM account. Then click Next to continue.
   - **If you are already registered:** you go straight to the issue-details page (see "avcrm-issue-details" for what to fill in).
5. On the issue-details page, choose a suitable Issue Category, select a Date/Time combination for issue creation, describe the issue details, and optionally upload compatible image or PDF attachments related to the issue (see "avcrm-issue-details" for attachment limits).
6. Click **Submit Issue**. Your issue is created with a ticket ID and associated with your account.

## [id: avcrm-issue-details, type: reference] What information do I need to provide when submitting an issue?
On the issue-details page you must:
- Choose a suitable Issue Category.
- Select a Date/Time combination for issue creation.
- Describe the issue details.
- Optionally upload compatible image or PDF attachments related to the issue — up to 3 attachments, each under 2MB.

## [id: avcrm-multiple-issues, type: reference] Can I create more than one issue from the same account?
Yes. Once your AV-CRM account exists, you can create more issues in the future from the same account.

## [id: avcrm-non-avsso-support, type: reference] What can I do if I'm not eligible to create a ticket myself (not registered in AVSSO)?
You can call support and ask them to create a ticket on your behalf.

## [id: avcrm-dashboard-tracking, type: reference] How can I see the issues I've submitted and their status?
Once you log in, you have access to a Dashboard that shows the issues you've submitted along with their current status.
